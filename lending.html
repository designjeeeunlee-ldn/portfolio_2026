The Solution

Three core interactions from the North Star prototype

01 · AI Launcher · Case Preparation

Proactive preparation inside the AI workspace

Launched alongside the credit application, the AI Launcher plans the case, pre-populates known client data, and surfaces policy recommendations so RMs can review and add context before submitting.

02 · AI Launcher · Document Intelligence

Financial & tenancy extraction with human sign-off

Inside the same AI workspace, raw client documents are extracted and compared against the Real Estate model—highlighting discrepancies for one-click RM approval or resolution.

03 · Narrative Creation

Evidence-backed narrative with inline AI editing

Generates a structured credit narrative complete with clickable source links for auditability and an inline prompt popover to weave in recent client context.

04 · AI Launcher · Multi-Agent Orchestration

Scalable hub-and-spoke AI workspace

Built on progressive disclosure—the AI Launcher keeps the active workflow focused in a collapsed rail and expands on demand to surface traceability tools and specialist sub-agents (e.g., Policy Agent, RM Assistant) that the lending orchestrator can invoke or be orchestrated by via a central RM orchestrator.
